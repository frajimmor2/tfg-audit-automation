Vulnerabilities

{{ n_vuln }} vulnerabilities where identified and {{ n_exploited }} of them where exploited. Each attack has been performed using an exploit from Metasploit. For every selected exploit, between one and three different payloads have been executed in order to increase the chances of a successful compromise and to evaluate different exploitation scenarios. This approach allows for a more comprehensive assessment of the target system’s vulnerabilities, as different payloads may trigger different behaviors or bypass certain protections. The payloads are only mentioned by their name in Metasploits, their content is not available in this report.

Some vulnerabilities identified during the audit did not receive any exploitation attempts. This is due to the fact that the Ollama models were unable to identify suitable exploits capable of targeting those specific vulnerabilities. As a result, although these vulnerabilities are present, they remain untested from an exploitation perspective within this assessment.
